A05 - Bracket Design

Objective

The objective is to detail design a bracket that holds a polyester strap and hangs from a rigid T-beam. The bracket has five features: Feature A is the cylinder the strap wraps around, Feature B is the plate that connects the cylinder to the block, and Features C, D and E are the dimensions of the T-slot in the block. Each feature was sized twice, once for strength (safety factor 4) and once for a 0.005 in deflection limit, and the reaction from each feature became the load on the next, starting at A.
